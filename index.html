Got Questions?

Common questions

What exactly do I get?

A digital wedding invitation page in the design you choose, personalised with your names, events, venue and message. You share it with guests as a single link.

Can I see the design before I pay?

Yes. Simply tap on any design image to open a full working live demo.

What do I need to fill in?

Bride and groom names, wedding date, time and venue. Parents’ names, other events, your story and a guest message are optional.

How do I add my photographs?

After payment, share your photographs with our team on WhatsApp. They are used only for your invitation.

How does payment work?

After you fill in your details you are taken to a secure Razorpay payment page. The price is the one shown on your design: ₹499 or ₹799.

What about cancellations and refunds?

Please read our Refund & Cancellation Policy, or ask us on WhatsApp.
